Operating guide
01 · Route

Click a circular switch to cycle straight, up, or down. Aim for the matching colored platform.

02 · Hold

Click a train to apply its brakes. Tap it again to release. Watch for following traffic.

03 · Repair

Select the repair tool, then a damaged switch. Repairs are free. Two reusable crews can work at once, and each repair takes four seconds.

04 · Emergency kit

Instantly fixes a damaged or jammed switch. You get one emergency kit per campaign level, or per 25 deliveries in Endless Mode; save it for urgent situations.

Mouse controls: Click a dispatch tool, then click switches to route or repair. Click a train to hold or release it and view its route. Use Start shift / Pause shift to control play. Complete Level 5 to unlock Endless Mode; it gets tougher every eight deliveries until difficulty caps.
